# Types of Tokenizable Assets

**Asset tokenization** opens the door to digitally representing a wide range of goods and property rights. These assets, traditionally limited by access barriers, can now be divided into smaller fractions and traded on blockchain platforms. Below we explore the main types of assets that can be tokenized and the specific benefits of each category.

### **1. Financial Assets**

* **Description**: Financial assets include stocks, bonds, and derivatives that represent ownership or the right to receive an economic return.
* **Benefits**:
  * **Liquidity**: Facilitates buying and selling in global markets and increases the liquidity of traditionally illiquid assets.
  * **Accessibility**: Allows retail investors to own fractions of shares or bonds from large companies.
* **Example**: The tokenization of bonds allows small investors to access government or corporate debt instruments with low initial amounts. Platforms like **Securitize** offer tokenization solutions for these assets, respecting local financial regulations.

### **2. Real Estate**

* **Description**: Real estate tokenization allows a physical property, such as a house or commercial building, to be divided into multiple tokens.
* **Benefits**:
  * **Fractionalization**: Enables partial investment in high-value properties, previously only accessible to large capitalists.
  * **Liquidity**: Facilitates the sale of property fractions in secondary markets without needing to sell the entire property.
* **Example**: An office tower in New York could be tokenized to allow investors from around the world to buy fractions of the property. Each token would represent a share in the property and the right to receive income generated by rents.

### **3. Commodities and Raw Materials**

* **Description**: The tokenization of commodities, such as gold, oil, or agricultural products, allows a digital representation of these physical resources.
* **Benefits**:
  * **Global Accessibility**: Enables the buying and selling of commodities without physical intermediaries and from anywhere in the world.
  * **Transparency**: The blockchain ensures a reliable record of transactions, minimizing fraud.
* **Example**: Tokenizing gold allows investors to buy fractions of the metal, stored in a vault, without needing to handle physical gold. Companies like **Paxos** already offer gold tokens, backed by a physical reserve.

### **4. Intellectual Property and Copyright**

* **Description**: Intellectual property, such as copyrights, patents, and trademarks, can be represented as tokens that represent usage rights or royalty income.
* **Benefits**:
  * **Income Generation**: Creators can sell fractions of their rights to future income.
  * **Access to New Investors**: Anyone interested in supporting creators or innovators can invest in their works.
* **Example**: An artist could tokenize their copyright on a song, allowing fans and investors to buy a portion of future income generated by plays and sales. Platforms like **Royalty Exchange** are already exploring this model on the blockchain.

### **5. Artistic Assets and Collectibles**

* **Description**: The tokenization of artworks, antiques, and collectibles allows owners to sell shares in these objects without transferring physical ownership.
* **Benefits**:
  * **Fractionalization**: High-value collectibles and artworks can be shared among many investors.
  * **Increased Liquidity**: Facilitates buying and selling on exchange platforms without risk of physical deterioration.
* **Example**: A high-value painting could be tokenized, so multiple people own a fraction of the work. This allows democratizing access to art investment, previously limited to major collectors.

### **6. Loan Contracts and Other Financial Instruments**

* **Description**: Loan contracts and other debt instruments can be tokenized, allowing collection rights to be divided into multiple parts.
* **Benefits**:
  * **Diversification**: Investors can acquire small parts of different loans, diversifying risk.
  * **Direct Access**: Allows lenders and borrowers to connect directly without banking intermediaries.
* **Example**: A loan contract for a small business can be divided into tokens, allowing multiple investors to finance the loan and receive interest. Companies like **Centrifuge** are integrating this type of asset into the DeFi ecosystem.

### **7. Carbon Assets and Energy Certificates**

* **Description**: The tokenization of carbon reduction certificates and energy credits allows trading these environmental assets on blockchain.
* **Benefits**:
  * **Transparency**: The blockchain ensures that records of carbon reduction or clean energy are reliable and auditable.
  * **Global Market**: Facilitates buying and selling in a global market without the need for intermediaries.
* **Example**: A renewable energy project can issue tokens representing its clean energy certificates. This allows companies to buy these tokens to offset their carbon emissions.
