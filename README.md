The best course to become an expert blockchain developer specialized in the Avalanche network!

Through 24 sessions, you will learn everything from blockchain fundamentals to creating your own Avalanche L1 blockchain and launching your own smart contracts on it.

Don't worry if you don't have any development or blockchain knowledge, we'll go step by step from scratch, and you'll have challenges after completing each session to put your knowledge into practice.
