# Creating Liquidity Pools on Uniswap

Creating liquidity pools on Uniswap allows users to facilitate exchanges on the platform by depositing token pairs into a pool. By doing so, they act as liquidity providers (LP) and receive a portion of the fees generated by exchanges in that pool. Let's see how to create a pool in both **Uniswap V2** and **Uniswap V3**.

### Creating Liquidity Pools on Uniswap

Creating liquidity pools on Uniswap allows users to facilitate exchanges on the platform by depositing token pairs into a pool. By doing so, they act as liquidity providers (LP) and receive a portion of the fees generated by exchanges in that pool. Let's see how to create a pool in both **Uniswap V2** and **Uniswap V3**.

#### **Creating a Pool in Uniswap V2:**

1. **Access the Uniswap Platform**: Visit [Uniswap](https://app.uniswap.org/) and connect your wallet (like MetaMask or Core) on the Sepolia network to interact with the platform.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (5).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2. **Select "Pool"**: From the main menu, select the "Pool" tab, then open the dropdown menu "More" and enter "V2 liquidity".

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (6).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. **Add V2 Liquidity**: Click on "Add V2 liquidity". This will allow you to start the process of creating a new pool or joining an existing one.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (7).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4. **Choose the Token Pair**: Enter the two tokens you want to deposit in the pool (for example, **ETH** and **USDC**). Make sure you have sufficient balance of both tokens in your wallet. You can even try it with the token you created in the "ERC20 Tokens" session.
5. **Define the Token Amount**: Uniswap V2 requires you to deposit equivalent amounts of each token (in terms of dollar value) to maintain pool balance. For example, if you want to deposit 1 ETH, you'll need to add the equivalent amount in USDC.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (8).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6. **Confirmation and Gas Fees**: Once you've set the amount, approve the amount of tokens you'll allow the contract to use, then click "Supply" to add liquidity. This action will generate a transaction and you'll need to pay a gas fee for the Uniswap contract to process the operation.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (9).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
7. **Receiving LP Tokens**: Upon completing the deposit, you'll receive **liquidity tokens (LP)**, which represent your share in the pool. These LP tokens will allow you to withdraw your share and earned fees whenever you wish.

#### **Creating a Pool in Uniswap V3:**

1. **Access and Connect your Wallet**: Same as in V2, start by accessing the Uniswap platform and connecting your wallet.
2. **Select "Pool"**: From the main menu, select the "Pool" tab, then enter "New position".

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (10).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3. **Choose the Token Pair**: In Uniswap V3, select the tokens you want to add to the pool (for example, **AVAX/USDC**).

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (11).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
4. **Choose the Fee Tier**: In Uniswap V3, you can select between different fee tiers (0.01%, 0.05%, 0.3%, and 1%) based on the risk and volatility of the token pair. Generally, more stable pairs like **USDC/USDT** have lower fees, while more volatile pairs, like **ETH/DAI**, may have higher fees.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (12).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
5. **Define the Price Range**: One of the main differences in V3 is **concentrated liquidity**. You'll need to specify a price range for each token where you want your liquidity to be active. For example, you can define a range of 15 USDC to 20 USDC for the AVAX token.

    * This range allows your liquidity to be used only within the selected prices. Outside this range, your liquidity will remain inactive until the price returns within these limits.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (13).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
6. **Define the Token Amount and Confirm the Transaction**: Enter the amounts of each token you want to contribute to the pool and confirm the transaction. Again, you'll receive LP tokens representing your share in the specified price range.

    <figure><img src="../.gitbook/assets/image (14).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
7. **Manage the Price Range**: If the price moves outside your range, you can adjust your liquidity manually or use services that adjust it automatically to maximize your returns.

### **Which Version of Uniswap to Choose?**

* **Uniswap V2** is ideal for LPs who want a more passive approach, without worrying about price range changes. It's a good option for those who prefer simplicity.
* **Uniswap V3** is suitable for experienced LPs looking to maximize returns through concentrated liquidity, although it requires more active management.
