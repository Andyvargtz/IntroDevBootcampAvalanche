# Decentralized Exchanges (DEX)

**Decentralized Exchanges (DEX)** are platforms that allow the buying and selling of cryptocurrencies **directly between users** without centralized intermediaries. Unlike traditional exchanges or **CEX** (Centralized Exchanges), DEXs eliminate the figure of the "custodian" that holds users' funds. Exchanges are conducted directly through smart contracts, maintaining transparency and security in each transaction.

### What is a DEX?

A DEX is a blockchain-built platform that allows users to **exchange tokens peer-to-peer**. DEXs operate in a decentralized and non-custodial manner, which means users maintain control of their assets at all times, operating directly from their wallets.

This decentralized approach eliminates the risk of fund loss that CEXs face in case of hacks or poor practices by the companies that manage them, as happened in incidents with centralized platforms like Mt. Gox or FTX. Instead of a company managing transactions and custody, DEXs operate through **smart contracts** that automate the exchange process on the blockchain, ensuring that transactions are executed only if the established conditions are met.

### **Types of DEX**

There are two main types of DEX that define how exchanges and liquidity are managed:

1. **Order Book-based DEX**: These function similarly to traditional centralized exchanges, using an order book where users post buy or sell orders at a specific price. Examples include **0x and dYdX**.
   * **Advantages**: Maintains the familiarity of order books and allows specific prices for each transaction.
   * **Disadvantages**: Requires sufficient liquidity and can be more complex to use.
2. **AMM-based DEX** (Automated Market Makers): Instead of an order book, these use **liquidity pools** where users (or liquidity providers) deposit token pairs. AMMs determine asset prices in the pool based on algorithms and mathematical formulas, eliminating the need for a specific order to perform exchanges. **Uniswap** and **SushiSwap** are examples of this type of DEX.
   * **Advantages**: Provide greater liquidity and allow uninterrupted operations.
   * **Disadvantages**: Prices fluctuate according to the token ratio in the pool, which can lead to impermanent losses for liquidity providers.

### **Advantages of DEX**

DEXs have several significant advantages that make them attractive, especially in the context of decentralized finance (DeFi):

* **Asset Control**: Users maintain total control over their funds and avoid third-party custody, minimizing risks of hacking and centralization.
* **Privacy and No KYC Verification**: Unlike CEXs, DEXs do not require users to complete identity verification processes (KYC), maintaining privacy.
* **Global Access and Decentralization**: Anyone with internet access and a wallet can participate, regardless of their geographic location or political restrictions.
